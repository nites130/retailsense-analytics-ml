03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more
perspective.

Is the demo based on real customers?

No. The built-in demo is seeded synthetic data, clearly labelled in the app. Import a real dataset such as UCI Online Retail before reporting real-data findings.

What does the model predict?

The probability of an existing customer purchasing again in the 30 days after the dataset cutoff. Probabilities are uncalibrated estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.

What kind of CSV can I upload?

A UTF-8 retail CSV with InvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, UnitPrice, CustomerID and Country. Use one currency; dashboard amounts are displayed in GBP. Download an example in Data studio.

Where is my data stored?

In your account’s SQLite database on the computer running RetailSense. Accounts are local to this installation; this edition is not a hosted cloud service.

Can I use this as a portfolio project?

Yes. The package includes source code, tests, documentation and a showcase guide. Understand the implementation and add your own analysis before describing your contribution.

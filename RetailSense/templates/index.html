PREDICTION LAB

What happens next?

Estimate whether an existing customer will buy again in the next 30 days.

METHOD

A test against time.

Historical customer activity becomes six features. Logistic Regression and Random Forest compete on a later validation window. The winner is evaluated on a separate, later test window.

  1. Learn from historyRecency, frequency, spend, tenure, average order, recent orders.
  2. Select on validationHighest F1 at a fixed 0.50 threshold.
  3. Evaluate on the futureNon-overlapping 30-day outcome windows; compare with a prior baseline.

MODEL STATUS

Ready when you are.

Load at least 180 days of data, then train. Demo data can be used to check the complete workflow; its scores do not establish real-world performance.

How to read these results

Precision: how often a predicted repeat buyer actually returns. Recall: how many returning buyers are found. F1 balances both. ROC AUC measures ranking; Brier score measures probability error (lower is better). These are historical estimates, not guaranteed outcomes or calibrated probabilities.

A customer can appear at several historical snapshots. This evaluates returning customers over time, not generalisation to completely unseen customers. Country filters do not affect model training.

Required columns

InvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, UnitPrice, CustomerID, Country

Dates: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS recommended. Rows with missing customer IDs, invalid dates, non-positive quantities or prices, and cancellation invoices are excluded. Exact duplicate rows are removed. Revenue is gross purchases for identified customers, not profit or net revenue.

For model evaluation, the maximum transaction date is assumed to be the last observed day. Only import a completed observation period. Your data is stored in your account’s local workspace.
